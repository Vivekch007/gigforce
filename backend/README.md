# GigForce – Contract & Gig Workforce Management Platform
 
## 📌 Overview
 
GigForce is a Contract and Gig Workforce Management Platform that helps organizations manage the complete lifecycle of contract workforce management.
 
The platform connects **Organizations, HR teams, Vendors, Contractors, Finance teams, and System Administrators** in a single system.
 
It manages the workflow from **workforce requisition and vendor sourcing to contractor selection, assignment, timesheet approval, and payment**.
 
---
 
## 🎯 Problem Statement
 
Organizations often depend on external vendors to find suitable contractors for temporary workforce requirements.
 
Managing requisitions, contractor profiles, interviews, assignments, tenure, timesheets, approvals, and payments across multiple stakeholders can become complex and difficult to track.
 
GigForce provides a centralized platform to manage this entire contractor lifecycle.
